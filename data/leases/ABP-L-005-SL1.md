**From:** Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd (the "Landlord")
**To:** Quillon Software Pte Ltd (the "Tenant")
**Date:** 1 October 2025
**Re:** Lease dated 10 July 2025 of #08-03, Harbourline Tower (the "Lease") — temporary consent to share occupation

## 1. Temporary Consent to Sublet {type=assignment_subletting modifies=6}

1.1 Notwithstanding Clause 6 of the Lease, the Landlord consents to the Tenant subletting or sharing occupation of part of the Premises not exceeding 630 square feet (30% of the lettable area) to its affiliate, **Quillon Labs Pte Ltd**, for the period from 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026 (the "Consent Period").

1.2 This consent is personal to Quillon Labs Pte Ltd. It does not extend to any other person or to any period after the Consent Period.

1.3 On expiry of the Consent Period, Clause 6 of the Lease applies again in full, and the Tenant shall ensure that Quillon Labs Pte Ltd has vacated the Premises.

## 2. Conditions {type=general}

2.1 The Tenant remains fully liable under the Lease for the acts and defaults of Quillon Labs Pte Ltd.

2.2 Save as set out in this letter, the Lease remains unchanged. Please countersign below to confirm your agreement.
