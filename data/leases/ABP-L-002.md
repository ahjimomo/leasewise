# Lease — Harbourline Tower, #22-01 to #22-04

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 30 May 2022 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Calderwood & Lim LLP** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Parties and Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant the whole of the 22nd storey of Harbourline Tower, 8 Harbourline Quay, Singapore, comprising units #22-01 to #22-04 (the "Premises"), with a lettable area of approximately 18,000 square feet.

## 2. Term and Commencement {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is five (5) years from 1 July 2022 to 30 June 2027 (both dates inclusive).

2.2 The Tenant shall have a rent-free fitting-out period of three (3) months from 1 July 2022.

## 3. Rent and Payment {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The rent is S$10.80 per square foot per month, amounting to S$194,400.00 per month (the "Rent"), exclusive of GST.

3.2 Rent shall be paid monthly in advance on or before the first day of each month without any deduction.

3.3 Interest at 8% per annum accrues on any sum unpaid after its due date.

## 4. Rent Review {type=rent_review}

4.1 The Rent shall be reviewed on 1 July 2025 to the open market rent of the Premises at that date, assuming vacant possession and a willing landlord and tenant.

4.2 The reviewed Rent shall not be lower than the Rent payable immediately before the review date. There is no upper limit on the increase.

4.3 The reviewed Rent, once agreed or determined, shall be recorded in a rent review memorandum signed by both parties.

## 5. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a monthly service charge, currently S$1.15 per square foot per month (S$20,700.00 per month), subject to revision by the Landlord from time to time on written notice.

## 6. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

6.1 The Tenant shall deposit with the Landlord a sum equal to three (3) months' Rent and service charge, being S$645,300.00, as security for the performance of its obligations.

6.2 Where the Rent or service charge is increased, the Tenant shall top up the deposit within fourteen (14) days so that it continues to equal three (3) months' Rent and service charge.

## 7. Option to Renew {type=renewal_option}

7.1 If the Tenant wishes to take a further lease of the Premises, and gives the Landlord written notice not less than nine (9) months and not more than twelve (12) months before expiry of the term, the Landlord shall grant a further term of five (5) years.

7.2 The rent for the further term shall be the prevailing market rent at the start of the further term, to be agreed between the parties. The further lease shall contain the same covenants as this Lease except this option to renew.

7.3 The option is personal to Calderwood & Lim LLP and conditional on there being no subsisting breach at the date of exercise.

## 8. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

8.1 The Tenant shall not assign this Lease. The Tenant may sublet part of the Premises to a firm or company associated with the Tenant with the Landlord's prior written consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 9. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

9.1 The Premises shall be used solely as offices for the practice of law and ancillary professional services.

## 10. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

10.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good and tenantable repair and condition, fair wear and tear excepted. The Landlord is responsible for the structure, common areas, and building services.

## 11. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

11.1 All fitting-out works require the Landlord's prior written approval and shall be carried out by contractors approved by the Landlord.

11.2 At the end of the term, the Tenant shall remove its fittings and reinstate the Premises to their original condition, except that the Tenant shall not be required to remove the internal staircase between levels 22 and 23 if the Tenant has by then leased level 23.

## 12. Insurance {type=insurance}

12.1 The Tenant shall take out public liability insurance for not less than S$5,000,000 and professional indemnity insurance as required by its regulator, and shall produce the policies on request.

## 13. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

13.1 Nothing in this Lease restricts the Landlord from selling the building. The Lease shall bind any purchaser. On completion of a sale, the Landlord may hand over the security deposit to the purchaser and be released from liability for it.

13.2 The Tenant shall, when requested, provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days.

## 14. Termination Events {type=termination}

14.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease by re-entry if rent is unpaid for fourteen (14) days after becoming due, if the Tenant fails to remedy any other breach within thirty (30) days of notice, or if the Tenant is wound up or becomes insolvent.

## 15. Stamp Duty and Costs {type=general}

15.1 Stamp duty on this Lease is payable by the Tenant.

## 16. Governing Law {type=general}

16.1 This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
