# Lease — Lumen Point Mall, #02-07

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 12 February 2024 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Lumière Optics Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant unit #02-07, Lumen Point Mall, 50 Lumen Point Avenue, Singapore (the "Premises"), with a lettable area of approximately 800 square feet.

## 2. Term {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is three (3) years from 1 March 2024 to 28 February 2027.

2.2 The Tenant shall have a rent-free fitting-out period of two (2) weeks from 1 March 2024.

## 3. Rent {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The Tenant shall pay a fixed monthly rent of S$24.00 per square foot per month, being S$19,200.00 per month, exclusive of GST, payable in advance on the first day of each month. There is no turnover rent.

## 4. Turnover Reporting {type=turnover_reporting}

4.1 Although no turnover rent is payable, the Tenant shall submit a monthly statement of its Gross Turnover from the Premises within ten (10) days after each month end, for the purposes of Clause 13 and for the Landlord's management of the mall.

## 5. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$1.90 per square foot per month (S$1,520.00 per month), revisable annually.

## 6. Advertising and Promotion Charge {type=service_charge}

6.1 The Tenant shall pay an advertising and promotion charge of S$0.80 per square foot per month (S$640.00 per month).

## 7. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

7.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of S$64,080.00, equal to three (3) months' rent, service charge, and advertising and promotion charge.

## 8. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

8.1 The Premises shall be used for the retail sale of spectacles, sunglasses, and contact lenses, and the provision of eye examinations by registered optometrists, under the trade name "Lumière Optics".

## 9. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

9.1 The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, or part with possession of the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent.

## 10. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

10.1 The Tenant shall fit out the Premises in accordance with the mall's fit-out manual. At the end of the term the Tenant shall reinstate the Premises to bare condition.

## 11. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

11.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises and its shopfront in good and clean condition and repair.

## 12. Insurance {type=insurance}

12.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$1,000,000 per occurrence.

## 13. Sales Performance Termination {type=break_option}

13.1 If the Tenant's Gross Turnover from the Premises in any period of twelve (12) consecutive months, starting on or after 1 March 2024, is less than S$600,000 (the "Sales Threshold"), the Tenant may terminate this Lease by giving the Landlord not less than three (3) months' written notice.

13.2 The notice must be given within two (2) months after the end of the twelve-month period in which the Tenant's Gross Turnover fell below the Sales Threshold, and must be accompanied by turnover statements for that period.

13.3 No termination fee is payable. The Tenant shall reinstate the Premises in accordance with Clause 10 on termination.

13.4 The Landlord may verify the turnover statements under Clause 4 before the termination takes effect.

## 14. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

14.1 The Landlord may sell Lumen Point Mall subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it. The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 15. Termination Events {type=termination}

15.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date, if any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 16. Retail Leasing Code {type=general}

16.1 The parties acknowledge that this Lease is a qualifying lease under the Lease Agreements for Retail Premises Act 2023 and has been prepared with reference to the Code of Conduct for Leasing of Retail Premises in Singapore.

## 17. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

17.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
