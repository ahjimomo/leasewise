# Lease — Kestrel Logistics Hub, #01-01 to #01-04

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 1 December 2022 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Larchmere Logistics (S) Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Definitions and Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 "Building" means Kestrel Logistics Hub, 12 Kestrel Crescent, Singapore. "Premises" means units #01-01 to #01-04 on the ground floor of the Building, with a combined lettable area of approximately 42,000 square feet, together with six (6) dedicated loading bays and the right to use the vehicle ramps and hardstanding in common with others.

1.2 The floor loading capacity of the Premises is 15 kN per square metre. The Tenant shall not exceed it.

## 2. Term {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is five (5) years from 1 January 2023 to 31 December 2027.

2.2 No rent-free period is granted.

## 3. Rent {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The initial rent is S$1.85 per square foot per month, being S$77,700.00 per month, exclusive of GST, payable monthly in advance.

3.2 The rent is adjusted annually under Clause 4.

## 4. Rent Review — Index-Linked {type=rent_review}

4.1 On 1 January of each year from 1 January 2024 (each a "Review Date"), the rent shall be adjusted by the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index (All Items) published by the Singapore Department of Statistics over the preceding calendar year (the "CPI Change").

4.2 The adjustment shall not be less than 0% (so the rent never decreases) and shall not exceed 5% in any one year.

4.3 The Landlord shall notify the Tenant in writing of the adjusted rent, with its calculation, within thirty (30) days after the CPI figures for the preceding year are published. Until then, the Tenant shall continue to pay the previous rent, and any balance shall be paid within fourteen (14) days of the notice.

## 5. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$0.35 per square foot per month (S$14,700.00 per month) towards the upkeep of the common areas, ramps, and security of the Building.

## 6. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

6.1 The Tenant shall provide a security deposit of S$277,200.00, equal to three (3) months' initial rent and service charge, by cash or banker's guarantee.

## 7. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

7.1 The Premises shall be used for warehousing and distribution of general goods, with ancillary offices not exceeding 15% of the lettable area. The Tenant may operate twenty-four (24) hours a day.

7.2 The Tenant shall not store dangerous goods, except in quantities exempt from licensing, without the Landlord's prior written consent.

## 8. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

8.1 The Tenant shall not assign this Lease. The Tenant may sublet up to 20% of the Premises to its customers for storage purposes with the Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 9. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

9.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises, including the roller shutters, dock levellers, and floor surface, in good repair, fair wear and tear excepted, and shall repair any damage caused by its forklifts and vehicles.

## 10. Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

10.1 At the end of the term, the Tenant shall remove its racking, mezzanines, and other installations and reinstate the Premises to their original condition, including making good the floor slab and any anchoring points.

## 11. Insurance {type=insurance}

11.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$5,000,000 per occurrence and insure all goods stored in the Premises.

## 12. Right of First Refusal {type=purchase_right}

12.1 If, during the term, the Landlord proposes to sell the Building, the Landlord shall first offer to sell the Building to the Tenant by written notice stating the price and principal terms (the "Offer").

12.2 The Tenant may accept the Offer by written notice within thirty (30) days. If the Tenant does not accept within that period, the Landlord may sell the Building to any third party within six (6) months, at a price not less than 95% of the price stated in the Offer and on terms not materially more favourable to the buyer.

12.3 If the Landlord does not complete a sale within that six-month period, this Clause applies again to any later proposed sale.

12.4 This Clause gives the Tenant a right of first refusal only. It does not give the Tenant an option to purchase the Building or any right to require the Landlord to sell.

12.5 This Clause does not apply to a transfer of the Building to a related corporation of the Landlord.

## 13. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

13.1 Subject to Clause 12, the Landlord may sell the Building subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it. The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 14. Termination Events {type=termination}

14.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date, if any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 15. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

15.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
