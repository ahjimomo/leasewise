# Lease — Kestrel Logistics Hub, #01-08

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 3 February 2025 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Polarvale Cold Storage Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Definitions and Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 "Building" means Kestrel Logistics Hub, 12 Kestrel Crescent, Singapore. "Premises" means unit #01-08 of the Building, with a lettable area of approximately 15,000 square feet, together with two (2) dedicated loading bays.

## 2. Term {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is three (3) years from 1 March 2025 to 29 February 2028.

2.2 The Tenant shall have a rent-free fitting-out period of three (3) months from 1 March 2025 to install its cold rooms.

## 3. Rent {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The rent is S$2.20 per square foot per month, being S$33,000.00 per month, exclusive of GST, payable monthly in advance. The rent is fixed for the term.

## 4. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$0.45 per square foot per month (S$6,750.00 per month), which includes a contribution to the Building's backup power supply.

4.2 Electricity consumed within the Premises is separately metered and paid by the Tenant.

## 5. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

5.1 The Tenant shall provide a security deposit of S$119,250.00, equal to three (3) months' rent and service charge.

## 6. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

6.1 The Premises shall be used for chilled and frozen storage and distribution of food products, with ancillary offices.

6.2 The Tenant shall comply with all food safety licensing requirements applicable to its operations.

## 7. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

7.1 The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, or part with possession of the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent.

## 8. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

8.1 The Tenant may install insulated cold rooms, refrigeration plant, and floor heating to prevent frost heave, in accordance with plans approved by the Landlord.

8.2 At the end of the term the Tenant shall remove the cold rooms and refrigeration plant and reinstate the Premises, including any repair to the floor slab caused by freezing.

## 9. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

9.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises and its refrigeration plant in good repair and shall maintain the floor heating system in continuous operation while the cold rooms are in use.

## 10. Insurance {type=insurance}

10.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$3,000,000 per occurrence and insurance of its stock against loss from refrigeration breakdown.

## 11. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

11.1 The Landlord may sell the Building subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it. The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 12. Termination Events {type=termination}

12.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date, if any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 13. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

13.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
