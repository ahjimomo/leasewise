# Lease — Harbourline Tower, #10-05

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 7 October 2024 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Quorvan Capital Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Parties and Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord lets and the Tenant takes #10-05, Harbourline Tower, 8 Harbourline Quay, Singapore (the "Premises"), with a lettable area of approximately 3,200 square feet.

## 2. Term and Commencement {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is three (3) years from 1 November 2024 to 31 October 2027.

2.2 The Tenant shall have a rent-free fitting-out period of one (1) month from 1 November 2024.

## 3. Rent and Payment {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The rent is S$11.50 per square foot per month, being S$36,800.00 per month (the "Rent"), exclusive of GST, fixed for the whole term.

3.2 Rent is payable monthly in advance on the first day of each month by GIRO.

3.3 Overdue sums bear interest at 8% per annum.

## 4. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$1.25 per square foot per month (S$4,000.00 per month), which the Landlord may revise annually by written notice.

## 5. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to six (6) months' Rent and service charge, being S$244,800.00, on signing this Lease.

5.2 The deposit is interest-free and refundable within thirty (30) days after the end of the term, less any amounts owing.

## 6. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

6.1 The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, part with, or share possession of the Premises or any part of it without the Landlord's prior written consent, which the Landlord may give or withhold in its absolute discretion.

## 7. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

7.1 The Premises shall be used as an office for fund management and investment advisory business only.

## 8. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

8.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good repair and condition, fair wear and tear excepted.

## 9. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

9.1 The Tenant's fitting-out works require the Landlord's prior written approval.

9.2 At the end of the term the Tenant shall reinstate the Premises to bare-shell condition at its own cost.

## 10. Insurance {type=insurance}

10.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$2,000,000 for any one occurrence.

## 11. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

11.1 The Landlord may sell the building subject to this Lease, and may transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it. The Tenant shall sign an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 12. Termination Events {type=termination}

12.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease by re-entry if: (a) rent is unpaid for fourteen (14) days after its due date; (b) the Tenant fails to remedy any other breach within thirty (30) days after notice; or (c) the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 13. Stamp Duty, Notices, and Governing Law {type=general}

13.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease.

13.2 Notices shall be in writing. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
