# Lease — Lumen Point Mall, #03-11

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 10 March 2025 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Sprout & Scholar Enrichment Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant unit #03-11, Lumen Point Mall, 50 Lumen Point Avenue, Singapore (the "Premises"), with a lettable area of approximately 2,400 square feet.

## 2. Term {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is three (3) years from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028.

2.2 The Tenant shall have a rent-free fitting-out period of one (1) month from 1 April 2025.

## 3. Rent {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The Tenant shall pay rent of S$14.00 per square foot per month, being S$33,600.00 per month, exclusive of GST, monthly in advance.

## 4. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$1.90 per square foot per month (S$4,560.00 per month), revisable annually on written notice.

## 5. Advertising and Promotion Charge {type=service_charge}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay an advertising and promotion charge of S$0.80 per square foot per month (S$1,920.00 per month), payable monthly together with the rent.

## 6. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

6.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of S$120,240.00, equal to three (3) months' rent, service charge, and advertising and promotion charge.

## 7. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

7.1 The Premises shall be used as a commercial school providing enrichment classes for children, including language, mathematics, and art classes, and for no other purpose.

7.2 The Tenant shall hold all registrations required for a private education provider and shall ensure that children are supervised at all times within the Premises.

## 8. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

8.1 The Tenant shall not assign or sublet the Premises. The Tenant may permit freelance instructors engaged by it to conduct classes at the Premises.

## 9. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

9.1 Fitting-out works, including classroom partitions and acoustic insulation, require the Landlord's prior written approval. At the end of the term the Tenant shall reinstate the Premises to bare condition.

## 10. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

10.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises in good, clean, and safe condition and repair.

## 11. Insurance {type=insurance}

11.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$3,000,000 per occurrence, covering injury to children attending classes.

## 12. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

12.1 The Landlord may sell Lumen Point Mall subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it. The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 13. Termination Events {type=termination}

13.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date, if the Tenant's registration as an education provider lapses, if any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 14. Retail Leasing Code {type=general}

14.1 The parties acknowledge that this Lease is a qualifying lease under the Lease Agreements for Retail Premises Act 2023 and has been prepared with reference to the Code of Conduct for Leasing of Retail Premises in Singapore.

## 15. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

15.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
