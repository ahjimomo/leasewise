# Lease — Lumen Point Mall, #B1-22

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 2 November 2023 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Golden Ladle Noodle House Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant unit #B1-22, Lumen Point Mall, 50 Lumen Point Avenue, Singapore (the "Premises"), having a lettable area of approximately 900 square feet.

## 2. Term {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is three (3) years from 1 December 2023 to 30 November 2026.

## 3. Rent {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The Tenant shall pay rent of S$30.00 per square foot per month, being S$27,000.00 per month, exclusive of GST, monthly in advance on the first day of each month.

3.2 Interest at 10% per annum is payable on any rent in arrears for more than seven (7) days.

## 4. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$1.90 per square foot per month (S$1,710.00 per month). The Landlord may vary the service charge at any time by written notice.

## 5. Advertising and Promotion Charge {type=service_charge}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay an advertising and promotion charge of S$0.80 per square foot per month (S$720.00 per month).

## 6. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

6.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of S$88,290.00, equal to three (3) months' rent, service charge, and advertising and promotion charge.

## 7. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

7.1 The Premises shall be used only as a noodle restaurant under the trade name "Golden Ladle", with the menu approved by the Landlord. Any material change to the menu requires the Landlord's prior written consent.

## 8. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

8.1 The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, or share possession of the Premises.

## 9. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

9.1 The Tenant takes the Premises with the previous tenant's kitchen exhaust and grease trap in place, in their present condition.

9.2 At the end of the term the Tenant shall reinstate the Premises to bare condition, including removal of the kitchen exhaust and grease trap, whether or not installed by the Tenant.

## 10. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

10.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises, the kitchen exhaust, and the grease trap clean and in good repair, and shall engage licensed pest control at least monthly.

## 11. Insurance {type=insurance}

11.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$1,000,000 per occurrence.

## 12. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

12.1 The Landlord may sell Lumen Point Mall subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it. The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within seven (7) days of request.

## 13. Termination Events {type=termination}

13.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid seven (7) days after its due date, if any other breach is not remedied within fourteen (14) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

13.2 The Landlord may terminate this Lease on three (3) months' written notice if the Landlord intends to carry out redevelopment or major asset enhancement works affecting the Premises. No compensation is payable to the Tenant.

## 14. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

14.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
