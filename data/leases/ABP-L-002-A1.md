# Amendment No. 1 to Lease — Harbourline Tower, #22-01 to #22-04

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Supplemental Agreement is made on 20 May 2026 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Calderwood & Lim LLP** (the "Tenant"), and varies the Lease dated 30 May 2022 (the "Lease").

## 1. Recitals {type=general}

1.1 Under Clause 4 of the Lease, the Rent was reviewed with effect from 1 July 2025 to S$11.30 per square foot per month (S$203,400.00 per month), as recorded in a rent review memorandum dated 12 June 2025.

1.2 The parties have agreed to extend the term of the Lease instead of the Tenant exercising the option to renew in Clause 7 of the Lease.

## 2. Extension of Term {type=term_commencement modifies=2}

2.1 The term of the Lease is extended by three (3) years. The term shall now expire on 30 June 2030.

## 3. Rent for the Extended Term {type=rent_payment modifies=3}

3.1 Until 30 June 2027, the Rent remains S$11.30 per square foot per month as recorded in the rent review memorandum.

3.2 From 1 July 2027 to 30 June 2030, the Rent shall be S$12.10 per square foot per month, amounting to S$217,800.00 per month, exclusive of GST. There shall be no further rent review during the extended term.

## 4. Option to Renew {type=renewal_option modifies=7}

4.1 Clause 7 (Option to Renew) of the Lease is deleted. The Tenant shall have no further option to renew.

## 5. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit modifies=6}

5.1 By 1 July 2027 the Tenant shall top up the security deposit under Clause 6.2 of the Lease to reflect the Rent payable from that date.

## 6. Continuation {type=general}

6.1 All other terms of the Lease continue unchanged. The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Supplemental Agreement.
