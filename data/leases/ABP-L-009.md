# Lease — Lumen Point Mall, #01-03

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 2 December 2024 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Pinewood Pharmacy Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant unit #01-03, Lumen Point Mall, 50 Lumen Point Avenue, Singapore (the "Premises"), with a lettable area of approximately 1,600 square feet.

## 2. Term {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is three (3) years from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2027.

2.2 The Tenant shall have a rent-free fitting-out period of one (1) month from 1 January 2025.

## 3. Rent {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The Tenant shall pay rent of S$22.00 per square foot per month, being S$35,200.00 per month, exclusive of GST, monthly in advance.

## 4. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$1.90 per square foot per month (S$3,040.00 per month), revisable annually on written notice.

## 5. Advertising and Promotion Charge {type=service_charge}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay an advertising and promotion charge of S$0.80 per square foot per month (S$1,280.00 per month).

## 6. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

6.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of S$118,560.00, equal to three (3) months' rent, service charge, and advertising and promotion charge.

## 7. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

7.1 The Premises shall be used as a retail pharmacy with a dispensary operated under a valid licence, together with the sale of health, wellness, and personal-care products.

7.2 The Tenant shall keep the dispensary open with a registered pharmacist in attendance at least from 10.00 am to 9.00 pm daily.

## 8. Exclusivity {type=exclusivity}

8.1 During the term, the Landlord shall not lease any other unit in Lumen Point Mall for use as a pharmacy or dispensary.

8.2 This Clause does not apply to: (a) the health and personal-care aisle within the anchor supermarket at #B1-01, provided no dispensing pharmacy is operated there; or (b) medical or dental clinics that dispense medicines to their own patients.

8.3 If the Landlord breaches this Clause, the Tenant's rent shall be reduced by 25% for so long as the breach continues, as the Tenant's sole remedy.

## 9. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

9.1 The Tenant may assign this Lease to a purchaser of its pharmacy business with the Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Subletting is not permitted.

## 10. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

10.1 The Tenant shall fit out the Premises, including a secure dispensary, in accordance with plans approved by the Landlord, and shall reinstate the Premises to bare condition at the end of the term.

## 11. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

11.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises and its fixtures in good repair.

## 12. Insurance {type=insurance}

12.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$2,000,000 and product liability insurance appropriate to a pharmacy.

## 13. Option to Renew {type=renewal_option}

13.1 The Tenant may renew for a further term of three (3) years by written notice given not less than six (6) months before expiry, at the prevailing market rent. Clause 8 (Exclusivity) shall continue in the renewed term.

## 14. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

14.1 The Landlord may sell Lumen Point Mall subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it. The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 15. Termination Events {type=termination}

15.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date, if the Tenant's pharmacy licence is revoked, if any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 16. Retail Leasing Code {type=general}

16.1 The parties acknowledge that this Lease is a qualifying lease under the Lease Agreements for Retail Premises Act 2023 and has been prepared with reference to the Code of Conduct for Leasing of Retail Premises in Singapore.

## 17. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

17.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
