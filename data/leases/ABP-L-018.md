# Lease — Atlas Bay Commons, #02-01

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 5 February 2024 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Pulsewell Fitness Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant unit #02-01, Atlas Bay Commons, 3 Atlas Bay Walk, Singapore (the "Premises"), being the whole of the second storey with a lettable area of approximately 7,800 square feet.

## 2. Term {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is five (5) years from 15 February 2024 to 14 February 2029.

2.2 The Tenant shall have a rent-free fitting-out period of three (3) months from 15 February 2024.

## 3. Rent {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The Tenant shall pay rent of S$6.50 per square foot per month, being S$50,700.00 per month, exclusive of GST, monthly in advance. The rent is fixed for the term.

## 4. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$1.60 per square foot per month (S$12,480.00 per month), revisable annually on written notice.

## 5. Advertising and Promotion Charge {type=service_charge}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay an advertising and promotion charge of S$0.60 per square foot per month (S$4,680.00 per month).

## 6. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

6.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of S$203,580.00, equal to three (3) months' rent, service charge, and advertising and promotion charge.

## 7. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

7.1 The Premises shall be used as a fitness centre and gymnasium, including group exercise classes and personal training, under the trade name "Pulsewell". The Tenant may operate twenty-four (24) hours a day.

7.2 The Tenant shall install acoustic and vibration-isolating flooring so that its activities do not disturb other occupiers.

## 8. Break Option {type=break_option}

8.1 The Tenant may terminate this Lease with effect from 15 February 2028 by giving the Landlord not less than six (6) months' prior written notice and paying a break fee equal to two (2) months' rent.

8.2 The break is conditional on the Tenant having paid all sums due and reinstated the Premises in accordance with Clause 10 by the break date.

## 9. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

9.1 The Tenant shall not assign or sublet the Premises. The Tenant may license personal trainers and a juice bar operator to provide services within the Premises.

## 10. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

10.1 The Tenant shall fit out the Premises, including shower and changing facilities, in accordance with plans approved by the Landlord.

10.2 At the end of the term the Tenant shall reinstate the Premises to bare condition, including removing the acoustic flooring and the shower and changing facilities, unless the Landlord elects to retain them.

## 11. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

11.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises, its equipment, and its wet areas in good repair and free from leaks.

## 12. Insurance {type=insurance}

12.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$5,000,000 per occurrence, covering injury to its members.

## 13. Landlord's Redevelopment Termination {type=termination}

13.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease by giving the Tenant not less than six (6) months' written notice if the Landlord intends to redevelop Atlas Bay Commons or carry out asset enhancement works that require vacant possession of the Premises.

13.2 If the Landlord terminates under this Clause, the Landlord shall refund to the Tenant the unamortised cost of the Tenant's fitting-out works, calculated on a straight-line basis over the term, up to a maximum of S$400,000.

13.3 **Declared deviation.** The parties acknowledge that this Clause deviates from the leasing principle on pre-termination by landlords due to redevelopment works in the Code of Conduct for Leasing of Retail Premises in Singapore, and have made a joint declaration recording that deviation.

## 14. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

14.1 The Landlord may sell Atlas Bay Commons subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it. The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 15. Termination Events {type=termination}

15.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date, if any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 16. Retail Leasing Code {type=general}

16.1 The parties acknowledge that this Lease is a qualifying lease under the Lease Agreements for Retail Premises Act 2023 and has been prepared with reference to the Code of Conduct for Leasing of Retail Premises in Singapore, subject to the declared deviation in Clause 13.3.

## 17. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

17.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
