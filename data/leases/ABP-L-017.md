# Lease — Atlas Bay Commons, #01-02

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 6 January 2025 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Tembusu Brew Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant unit #01-02, Atlas Bay Commons, 3 Atlas Bay Walk, Singapore (the "Premises"), with a lettable area of approximately 950 square feet, and a licence to use an outdoor seating area of about 120 square feet directly in front of the Premises.

## 2. Term {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is three (3) years from 1 February 2025 to 31 January 2028.

2.2 The Tenant shall have a rent-free fitting-out period of three (3) weeks from 1 February 2025.

## 3. Rent {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The Tenant shall pay monthly in advance the higher of: (a) Base Rent of S$26.00 per square foot per month, being S$24,700.00 per month; and (b) Turnover Rent of 8% of the Tenant's Gross Turnover for that month.

3.2 Any Turnover Rent in excess of the Base Rent is payable within fourteen (14) days after the monthly turnover statement is due.

3.3 All sums are exclusive of GST. Late payment bears interest at 8% per annum.

## 4. Turnover Reporting {type=turnover_reporting}

4.1 The Tenant shall connect its point-of-sale system to the Landlord's sales-data interface, and shall submit a monthly statement of Gross Turnover within seven (7) days after each month end.

4.2 The Tenant shall submit an annual Gross Turnover statement certified by a public accountant within ninety (90) days after each financial year end.

4.3 The Landlord shall keep the Tenant's sales data confidential and use it only to administer this Lease and manage the building.

## 5. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$1.60 per square foot per month (S$1,520.00 per month), revisable annually by the Landlord on written notice.

## 6. Advertising and Promotion Charge {type=service_charge}

6.1 The Tenant shall pay an advertising and promotion charge of S$0.60 per square foot per month (S$570.00 per month), applied by the Landlord towards marketing the retail podium.

## 7. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

7.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of S$80,370.00, being three (3) months' Base Rent, service charge, and advertising and promotion charge.

## 8. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

8.1 The Premises shall be used only as a café serving coffee, tea, light meals, and pastries under the trade name "Tembusu Brew". The Tenant may sell beer and wine with the Landlord's consent and a valid liquor licence.

## 9. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

9.1 The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, or share possession of the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent.

## 10. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

10.1 Fitting-out works must comply with the building's fit-out manual, including installation of an exhaust system and grease trap approved by the Landlord.

10.2 At the end of the term, the Tenant shall reinstate the Premises to bare condition, removing its kitchen exhaust ducting and sealing all openings.

## 11. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

11.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises, its kitchen equipment, and its grease trap clean and in good repair, and shall engage licensed pest control at least monthly.

## 12. Insurance {type=insurance}

12.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$2,000,000 per occurrence, naming the Landlord as an additional insured.

## 13. Option to Renew {type=renewal_option}

13.1 If the Tenant gives written notice not less than six (6) months before the end of the term and is not in breach, the Landlord shall grant a further term of three (3) years at the prevailing market rent, with the Turnover Rent percentage to be agreed.

## 14. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

14.1 The Landlord may sell Atlas Bay Commons subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it. The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 15. Termination Events {type=termination}

15.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if: (a) rent is unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date; (b) the Tenant ceases to trade from the Premises for more than fourteen (14) consecutive days without consent; (c) any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice; or (d) the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 16. Retail Leasing Code {type=general}

16.1 The parties acknowledge that this Lease is a qualifying lease under the Lease Agreements for Retail Premises Act 2023 and has been prepared with reference to the Code of Conduct for Leasing of Retail Premises in Singapore.

## 17. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

17.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
