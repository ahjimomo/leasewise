## 3. Rent for the Renewed Term {type=rent_payment modifies=3}

3.1 From 1 May 2026, the Rent is S$11.40 per square foot per month, being S$74,100.00 per month, exclusive of GST.

4.2 The service charge payable from 1 May 2026 is S$1.20 per square foot per month, which the Landlord may revise annually up to the revised cap.

5.1 The security deposit is increased to S$245,700.00, being three (3) months' Rent and service charge at the rates applying from 1 May 2026. The Tenant shall pay the difference by 1 May 2026.

6.1 All other terms of the Lease apply to the renewed term. The Tenant shall bear the stamp duty on this Supplemental Agreement.
