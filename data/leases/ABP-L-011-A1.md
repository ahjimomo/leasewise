# Amendment No. 1 — Lumen Point Mall, #B1-22

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Supplemental Agreement is made on 26 May 2025 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Golden Ladle Noodle House Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant"), and varies the Lease dated 2 November 2023 (the "Lease").

## 1. Background {type=general}

1.1 Footfall in the basement of Lumen Point Mall has been reduced by the closure of the mall's basement link to the transport interchange for upgrading works. The parties have agreed a temporary rent reduction.

## 2. Temporary Rent Reduction {type=rent_payment modifies=3}

2.1 For the period from 1 June 2025 to 31 May 2026 (the "Relief Period"), the rent under Clause 3.1 of the Lease is reduced to S$26.00 per square foot per month, being S$23,400.00 per month, exclusive of GST.

2.2 From 1 June 2026, the rent reverts to S$30.00 per square foot per month as provided in Clause 3.1 of the Lease.

2.3 If the Tenant is more than fourteen (14) days late in paying any rent during the Relief Period, the reduction ends immediately and the full rent is payable from the start of the Relief Period.

## 3. Continuation {type=general}

3.1 The service charge, advertising and promotion charge, and security deposit are not affected. Save as amended above, the Lease remains in full force.
