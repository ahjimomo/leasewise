# Lease — Lumen Point Mall, #01-20

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 5 August 2024 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Urban Stride Footwear Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant unit #01-20, Lumen Point Mall, 50 Lumen Point Avenue, Singapore (the "Premises"), with a lettable area of approximately 1,300 square feet.

## 2. Term {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is three (3) years from 1 September 2024 to 31 August 2027.

2.2 The Tenant shall have a rent-free fitting-out period of two (2) weeks from 1 September 2024.

## 3. Rent {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The Tenant shall pay rent of S$20.00 per square foot per month, being S$26,000.00 per month, exclusive of GST, monthly in advance.

## 4. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$1.90 per square foot per month (S$2,470.00 per month), revisable annually on written notice.

## 5. Advertising and Promotion Charge {type=service_charge}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay an advertising and promotion charge of S$0.80 per square foot per month (S$1,040.00 per month).

## 6. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

6.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of S$88,530.00, equal to three (3) months' rent, service charge, and advertising and promotion charge.

## 7. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

7.1 The Premises shall be used for the retail sale of footwear, socks, and shoe-care products under the trade name "Urban Stride".

## 8. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

8.1 The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, or part with possession of the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent.

## 9. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

9.1 The Tenant shall fit out the Premises in accordance with the mall's fit-out manual and reinstate the Premises to bare condition at the end of the term.

## 10. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

10.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises and its shopfront in good and clean condition and repair.

## 11. Insurance {type=insurance}

11.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$1,000,000 per occurrence.

## 12. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

12.1 The Landlord may sell Lumen Point Mall subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it. The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 13. Termination Events {type=termination}

13.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date, if any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 14. Retail Leasing Code {type=general}

14.1 The parties acknowledge that this Lease is a qualifying lease under the Lease Agreements for Retail Premises Act 2023 and has been prepared with reference to the Code of Conduct for Leasing of Retail Premises in Singapore.

## 15. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

15.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
