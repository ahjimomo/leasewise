# Lease — Kestrel Logistics Hub, #03-02

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 13 October 2025 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Tidewater Packaging Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Definitions and Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 "Building" means Kestrel Logistics Hub, 12 Kestrel Crescent, Singapore. "Premises" means unit #03-02 of the Building, with a lettable area of approximately 5,200 square feet.

## 2. Term {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is two (2) years from 1 November 2025 to 31 October 2027. There is no option to renew.

## 3. Rent {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The rent is S$1.95 per square foot per month, being S$10,140.00 per month, exclusive of GST, payable monthly in advance and fixed for the term.

## 4. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$0.35 per square foot per month (S$1,820.00 per month).

## 5. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

5.1 The Tenant shall provide a security deposit of S$35,880.00, equal to three (3) months' rent and service charge.

## 6. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

6.1 The Premises shall be used for the storage, cutting, and assembly of paper and plastic packaging materials, with ancillary offices.

## 7. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

7.1 The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, or part with possession of the Premises.

## 8. Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

8.1 At the end of the term the Tenant shall remove all its machinery, racking, and stock, and return the Premises clean and in the condition in which they were handed over.

## 9. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

9.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises in good repair, and shall keep combustible materials stored in accordance with fire safety requirements.

## 10. Insurance {type=insurance}

10.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$1,000,000 per occurrence and fire insurance on its stock.

## 11. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

11.1 The Landlord may sell the Building subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it.

## 12. Termination Events {type=termination}

12.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date, if any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 13. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

13.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
