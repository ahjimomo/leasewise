**Re:** Lease dated 12 May 2025 of #05-03, Atlas Bay Commons (the "Lease") — service charge

## 1. Service Charge Freeze {type=service_charge modifies=4}

1.1 Notwithstanding Clause 4.2 of the Lease, the service charge shall remain at S$1.10 per square foot per month for the period from 1 June 2025 to 31 May 2027 (the "Freeze Period"). The increase that would otherwise apply on 1 June 2026 is waived.

1.2 From 1 June 2027, the annual 3% increase under Clause 4.2 of the Lease resumes, applied to the frozen rate of S$1.10. The service charge from 1 June 2027 will therefore be approximately S$1.133 per square foot per month.

1.3 This concession is personal to the Tenant and ends if the Lease is assigned.

## 2. General {type=general}
