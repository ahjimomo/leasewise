# Lease — Harbourline Tower, #15-02

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 3 April 2023 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Verdance Health Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Parties and Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant #15-02, Harbourline Tower, 8 Harbourline Quay, Singapore (the "Premises"), having a lettable area of approximately 6,500 square feet.

## 2. Term and Commencement {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is three (3) years commencing on 1 May 2023 and expiring on 30 April 2026.

2.2 The Tenant is granted a rent-free fitting-out period of one (1) month from 1 May 2023.

## 3. Rent and Payment {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The Tenant shall pay rent of S$10.50 per square foot per month, being S$68,250.00 per month (the "Rent"), exclusive of GST.

3.2 Rent is payable monthly in advance on the first day of each month, without deduction or set-off.

3.3 Interest at 8% per annum is payable on overdue sums.

## 4. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge, initially S$1.15 per square foot per month (S$7,475.00 per month).

4.2 The Landlord may revise the service charge annually, provided that during the term the service charge shall not exceed S$1.20 per square foot per month (the "Service Charge Cap").

## 5. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to three (3) months' Rent and service charge, being S$227,175.00, on signing this Lease. The deposit bears no interest.

## 6. Option to Renew {type=renewal_option}

6.1 Provided the Tenant has paid the rent and performed its obligations, the Landlord shall, on the Tenant's written request made not less than six (6) months before expiry, grant a further term of three (3) years at the prevailing market rent.

6.2 The renewed lease shall contain the same terms as this Lease, except for this option to renew and the rent-free period.

## 7. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

7.1 The Tenant shall not assign this Lease. The Tenant may sublet part of the Premises only with the Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 8. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

8.1 The Premises shall be used as the corporate office of the Tenant's healthcare technology business. No clinical, diagnostic, or patient-facing services may be provided at the Premises.

## 9. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

9.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good and tenantable repair, fair wear and tear excepted. The Landlord maintains the structure and common building services.

## 10. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

10.1 Fitting-out works require the Landlord's prior written approval of plans and contractors.

10.2 On expiry or termination, the Tenant shall reinstate the Premises to bare-shell condition, unless the Landlord agrees in writing otherwise.

## 11. Insurance {type=insurance}

11.1 The Tenant shall insure against public liability for at least S$3,000,000 per occurrence and insure its own contents.

## 12. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

12.1 The Landlord may sell or transfer the building subject to this Lease, and may transfer the security deposit to the purchaser and thereby be released from liability for it.

12.2 The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of the Landlord's request.

## 13. Termination Events {type=termination}

13.1 The Landlord may re-enter and terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date, if any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice, or on the Tenant's insolvency.

## 14. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

14.1 Stamp duty on this Lease shall be borne by the Tenant. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
