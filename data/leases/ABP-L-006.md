# Lease — Lumen Point Mall, #B1-01 (Anchor Supermarket)

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 15 November 2021 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Pantrywell Supermarkets Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant unit #B1-01, Lumen Point Mall, 50 Lumen Point Avenue, Singapore (the "Premises"), with a lettable area of approximately 28,000 square feet, together with the right to use the loading bay and the basement goods lift in common with other tenants.

## 2. Term {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is six (6) years from 1 January 2022 to 31 December 2027.

2.2 The Tenant shall have a rent-free fitting-out period of four (4) months from 1 January 2022.

## 3. Rent {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The Tenant shall pay monthly, in advance, the higher of: (a) the Base Rent; and (b) the Turnover Rent.

3.2 The Base Rent is:
- from 1 January 2022 to 31 December 2024: S$9.50 per square foot per month (S$266,000.00 per month); and
- from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2027: S$10.20 per square foot per month (S$285,600.00 per month).

3.3 The Turnover Rent is 1.5% of the Tenant's Gross Turnover for the month. Any excess of the Turnover Rent over the Base Rent is payable within fourteen (14) days after the Tenant delivers its monthly turnover statement.

3.4 All sums are exclusive of GST.

## 4. Turnover Reporting and Audit {type=turnover_reporting}

4.1 The Tenant shall deliver to the Landlord, within ten (10) days after the end of each month, a statement of its Gross Turnover from the Premises for that month, certified by a director.

4.2 Within three (3) months after the end of each financial year, the Tenant shall deliver a statement of annual Gross Turnover certified by its external auditors.

4.3 The Landlord may, on fourteen (14) days' notice, audit the Tenant's sales records. If the audit reveals an under-declaration of more than 2%, the Tenant shall pay the cost of the audit in addition to any shortfall in Turnover Rent.

4.4 "Gross Turnover" means all sales made at or from the Premises, including online orders fulfilled from the Premises, excluding GST and refunds actually given.

## 5. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$1.20 per square foot per month (S$33,600.00 per month). The Landlord may revise the service charge annually, provided any increase does not exceed 5% in any year.

5.2 The Tenant shall not be required to pay any advertising and promotion charge.

## 6. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

6.1 The Tenant shall provide a security deposit of S$898,800.00, equal to three (3) months' initial Base Rent and service charge, by cash or banker's guarantee.

## 7. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

7.1 The Premises shall be used as a supermarket for the retail sale of groceries, fresh produce, household goods, and a health and personal-care aisle, under the trade name "Pantrywell".

7.2 The health and personal-care aisle may sell over-the-counter medicines, but the Tenant shall not operate a dispensing pharmacy at the Premises.

## 8. Exclusivity {type=exclusivity}

8.1 During the term, the Landlord shall not lease or permit the use of any other part of Lumen Point Mall exceeding 2,000 square feet as a supermarket, hypermarket, or grocery store.

8.2 This Clause does not restrict: (a) convenience stores of not more than 1,500 square feet; (b) specialty food retailers such as bakeries, confectioners, and wine shops; or (c) food and beverage outlets.

8.3 This Clause binds any purchaser or transferee of Lumen Point Mall (see Clause 15).

## 9. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

9.1 The Tenant shall not assign this Lease without the Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld in the case of an assignment to a related corporation of the Tenant.

9.2 The Tenant may grant concession or counter licences within the Premises covering in total not more than 10% of the lettable area without consent.

## 10. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

10.1 The Tenant shall fit out the Premises at its own cost, including cold rooms, refrigeration, and checkout counters, in accordance with plans approved by the Landlord.

10.2 At the end of the term the Tenant shall remove its refrigeration plant and fittings and reinstate the Premises to the condition in which they were handed over, with all floor penetrations properly sealed.

## 11. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

11.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises, its refrigeration plant, and its grease traps in good working order and repair.

11.2 The Landlord shall maintain the structure, the common areas, and the mall's central services.

## 12. Insurance {type=insurance}

12.1 The Tenant shall insure against public liability for not less than S$10,000,000 per occurrence, and insure its stock, plant, and fittings against fire, flood, and spoilage.

## 13. Opening Hours {type=general}

13.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises open for trade at least from 8.00 am to 10.00 pm every day, including public holidays.

## 14. Option to Renew {type=renewal_option}

14.1 The Tenant may, by written notice given not less than twelve (12) months before the end of the term, require the Landlord to grant a further lease of six (6) years from 1 January 2028.

14.2 The Base Rent for the further term shall be the prevailing market rent, not exceeding 115% of the Base Rent payable in the final year of the term. The Turnover Rent percentage shall remain 1.5%.

## 15. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

15.1 The Landlord may sell Lumen Point Mall subject to this Lease. The Landlord shall procure that any purchaser covenants directly with the Tenant to observe the Landlord's obligations in this Lease, including Clause 8 (Exclusivity).

15.2 On completion of the sale, the Landlord may transfer the security deposit to the purchaser and be released from liability for it.

15.3 The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 16. Termination Events {type=termination}

16.1 The Landlord may re-enter and terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid twenty-one (21) days after its due date, if any other material breach is not remedied within sixty (60) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 17. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

17.1 Stamp duty on this Lease is payable by the Tenant. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
