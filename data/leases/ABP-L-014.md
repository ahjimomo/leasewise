# Lease — Kestrel Logistics Hub, #02-05

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 4 June 2024 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Halvorne Precision Engineering Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Definitions and Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 "Building" means Kestrel Logistics Hub, 12 Kestrel Crescent, Singapore. "Premises" means unit #02-05 of the Building, with a lettable area of approximately 9,600 square feet, served by the Building's cargo lift.

## 2. Term {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is five (5) years from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2029.

2.2 The Tenant shall have a rent-free fitting-out period of two (2) months from 1 July 2024.

## 3. Rent {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The rent is S$2.10 per square foot per month, being S$20,160.00 per month, exclusive of GST, payable monthly in advance. The rent is fixed for the term.

## 4. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$0.35 per square foot per month (S$3,360.00 per month).

## 5. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

5.1 The Tenant shall provide a security deposit of S$70,560.00, equal to three (3) months' rent and service charge.

## 6. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

6.1 The Premises shall be used for precision machining, light assembly, and quality testing of engineering components, with ancillary offices.

6.2 The Tenant shall comply with all noise and vibration limits set by the Landlord and the authorities, and shall obtain the Landlord's consent before installing any machine weighing more than 5 tonnes.

## 7. Break Option {type=break_option}

7.1 The Tenant may terminate this Lease with effect from 1 July 2027 (the "Break Date") by giving the Landlord not less than six (6) months' prior written notice.

7.2 The break is conditional on the Tenant having paid all sums due and on the Tenant completing reinstatement under Clause 10 by the Break Date. If reinstatement is not complete, the Lease shall continue.

7.3 No break fee is payable, but the Tenant shall repay the rent waived during the fitting-out period.

## 8. Option to Renew {type=renewal_option}

8.1 The Tenant may renew this Lease for a further term of three (3) years by written notice given not less than six (6) months before expiry, at the prevailing market rent.

## 9. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

9.1 The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, or part with possession of the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent.

## 10. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

10.1 The Tenant may install machinery, compressed-air lines, three-phase power, and fume extraction in accordance with plans approved by the Landlord.

10.2 At the end of the term or on earlier termination, the Tenant shall, at its own cost: (a) remove all machinery, plinths, and services installed by it; (b) repair and level the floor slab; (c) seal all wall and roof penetrations; and (d) provide the Landlord with a certificate from a professional engineer confirming that the floor slab and structure have not been damaged.

## 11. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

11.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises and its installations in good repair and shall promptly repair any damage to the Building caused by vibration from its machinery.

## 12. Insurance {type=insurance}

12.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$3,000,000 per occurrence and insurance of its machinery and stock.

## 13. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

13.1 The Landlord may sell the Building subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it. The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 14. Termination Events {type=termination}

14.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date, if any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 15. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

15.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
