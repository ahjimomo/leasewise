# Amendment No. 1 to Lease — Harbourline Tower, #18-01

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Supplemental Agreement is made on 15 September 2025 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Larchmere Analytics Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant"), and is supplemental to the Lease dated 5 February 2024 for #18-01, Harbourline Tower (the "Lease").

## 1. Background {type=general}

1.1 The Tenant has requested additional rent-free concessions, and the Landlord has agreed, in exchange for the Tenant giving up its right to terminate the Lease early.

1.2 Capitalised terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Lease.

## 2. Deletion of Break Option {type=break_option modifies=7}

2.1 With effect from the date of this Supplemental Agreement, Clause 7 (Break Option) of the Lease is deleted in its entirety. The Tenant shall have no right to terminate the Lease before 28 February 2030 except as expressly provided in the Lease.

2.2 Clause 7.3 of the Lease (repayment of rent-free period on exercise of the break) accordingly ceases to apply.

## 3. Additional Rent-Free Period {type=rent_payment modifies=3}

3.1 In consideration of the deletion of the break option, the Landlord waives the Rent (but not the Service Charge) for the period from 1 October 2025 to 30 November 2025 (two (2) months).

3.2 From 1 December 2025, Rent is payable at the rate set out in Clause 3.1 of the Lease, subject to review under Clause 4 of the Lease.

## 4. Continuation {type=general}

4.1 Save as amended by this Supplemental Agreement, the Lease remains in full force and effect. The Tenant shall bear the stamp duty (if any) on this Supplemental Agreement.
