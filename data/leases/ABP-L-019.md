# Lease — Atlas Bay Commons, #05-03

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 12 May 2025 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Saltmarsh Design Studio Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Parties and Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant #05-03, Atlas Bay Commons, 3 Atlas Bay Walk, Singapore (the "Premises"), an office unit with a lettable area of approximately 1,800 square feet.

## 2. Term and Commencement {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is three (3) years from 1 June 2025 to 31 May 2028.

2.2 The Tenant shall have a rent-free fitting-out period of one (1) month from 1 June 2025.

## 3. Rent and Payment {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The rent is S$8.90 per square foot per month, being S$16,020.00 per month, exclusive of GST, payable monthly in advance and fixed for the term.

## 4. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge, initially S$1.10 per square foot per month (S$1,980.00 per month).

4.2 The service charge shall increase by 3% on 1 June 2026 and on each 1 June after that, compounded annually.

## 5. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of S$54,000.00, equal to three (3) months' rent and initial service charge.

## 6. Option to Renew {type=renewal_option}

6.1 The Tenant may renew this Lease for a further term of three (3) years by written notice given not less than six (6) months before expiry, at the prevailing market rent.

## 7. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

7.1 The Tenant shall not assign this Lease. The Tenant may share the Premises with not more than two (2) freelance designers working on the Tenant's projects, without the Landlord's consent.

## 8. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

8.1 The Premises shall be used as a design studio and office, including the display of design samples to clients by appointment. No retail sales may be made from the Premises.

## 9. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

9.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good and tenantable repair, fair wear and tear excepted.

## 10. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

10.1 Fitting-out works require the Landlord's prior written approval. At the end of the term the Tenant shall reinstate the Premises to bare-shell condition.

## 11. Insurance {type=insurance}

11.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$1,000,000 per occurrence.

## 12. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

12.1 The Landlord may sell Atlas Bay Commons subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it. The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 13. Termination Events {type=termination}

13.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date, if any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 14. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

14.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
