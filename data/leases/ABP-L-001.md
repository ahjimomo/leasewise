# Lease — Harbourline Tower, #18-01

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 5 February 2024 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Larchmere Analytics Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Parties and Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant the premises known as #18-01, Harbourline Tower, 8 Harbourline Quay, Singapore (the "Premises"), having a lettable area of approximately 12,400 square feet, together with the right to use the common areas of the building.

1.2 The lettable area stated above is agreed for all purposes of this Lease and will not be re-measured.

## 2. Term and Commencement {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is six (6) years, commencing on 1 March 2024 (the "Commencement Date") and expiring on 28 February 2030, unless terminated earlier under this Lease.

2.2 The Landlord grants the Tenant a rent-free fitting-out period of two (2) months from the Commencement Date, during which Rent (but not Service Charge) is waived.

## 3. Rent and Payment {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The Tenant shall pay rent of S$11.20 per square foot per month, being S$138,880.00 per month (the "Rent"), exclusive of GST.

3.2 Rent is payable monthly in advance on the first day of each calendar month by GIRO or bank transfer, without deduction or set-off.

3.3 Overdue sums bear interest at 8% per annum from the due date until payment.

3.4 GST at the prevailing rate is payable on the Rent, Service Charge, and all other sums payable under this Lease.

## 4. Rent Review {type=rent_review}

4.1 The Rent shall be reviewed with effect from 1 March 2027 (the "Review Date") to the prevailing market rent for comparable Grade A office premises in the vicinity.

4.2 The reviewed Rent shall not be less than the Rent payable immediately before the Review Date, nor more than 115% of it.

4.3 If the parties have not agreed the reviewed Rent three (3) months before the Review Date, either party may refer the matter to an independent valuer appointed by the President of the Singapore Institute of Surveyors and Valuers, acting as expert and not as arbitrator.

## 5. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge, currently S$1.25 per square foot per month, being S$15,500.00 per month (the "Service Charge"), towards the cost of operating, maintaining, and insuring the building.

5.2 The Landlord may revise the Service Charge once in each calendar year by giving the Tenant not less than one (1) month's written notice.

## 6. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

6.1 On signing this Lease, the Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to three (3) months' Rent and Service Charge, being S$463,140.00 (the "Deposit"). The Deposit may be provided by banker's guarantee in a form approved by the Landlord.

6.2 The Deposit bears no interest and shall be refunded within thirty (30) days after expiry of the term and the Tenant's reinstatement of the Premises, less any sums owing.

## 7. Break Option {type=break_option}

7.1 The Tenant may terminate this Lease with effect from 1 March 2027 (the "Break Date") by giving the Landlord not less than six (6) months' prior written notice.

7.2 The break is conditional on: (a) the Tenant having paid all Rent and Service Charge due up to the Break Date; (b) the Tenant paying a break fee equal to three (3) months' Rent; and (c) the Tenant yielding up the Premises reinstated in accordance with Clause 11.

7.3 If the Tenant validly exercises this option, the rent-free period granted under Clause 2.2 shall be repaid to the Landlord on a pro rata basis.

## 8. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

8.1 The Tenant shall not assign, mortgage, or charge this Lease.

8.2 The Tenant may sublet or share occupation of part (but not the whole) of the Premises, not exceeding 25% of the lettable area, with the Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 9. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

9.1 The Premises shall be used only as an office for the Tenant's business of data analytics and software consultancy, and for no other purpose.

## 10. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

10.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises, including its fixtures and the Landlord's installations within the Premises, in good and tenantable repair, fair wear and tear excepted.

10.2 The Landlord shall maintain the structure, roof, exterior, lifts, and central air-conditioning plant of the building.

## 11. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

11.1 The Tenant shall carry out its fitting-out works in accordance with plans approved by the Landlord and the Landlord's fit-out guidelines, at the Tenant's cost.

11.2 On expiry or earlier termination, the Tenant shall reinstate the Premises to bare-shell condition as at the Commencement Date, at its own cost, unless the Landlord elects in writing to accept the Premises with some or all of the Tenant's fittings.

## 12. Insurance {type=insurance}

12.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$5,000,000 for any one occurrence, and insurance of its fixtures, fittings, and contents for their full replacement value.

12.2 The Landlord shall insure the building against fire and other usual risks.

## 13. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

13.1 The Landlord may sell or transfer its interest in the building. This Lease shall continue in force and bind the purchaser.

13.2 On such transfer, the Landlord may transfer the Deposit to the purchaser, whereupon the Landlord shall be released from its obligation to refund the Deposit.

13.3 Within fourteen (14) days of the Landlord's request, the Tenant shall sign an estoppel certificate confirming the term, the Rent, the Deposit held, and whether any breach is outstanding.

## 14. Termination Events {type=termination}

14.1 The Landlord may re-enter the Premises and terminate this Lease if: (a) any Rent or Service Charge remains unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date; (b) the Tenant breaches any other obligation and fails to remedy the breach within thirty (30) days of written notice; or (c) the Tenant enters liquidation, judicial management, or makes an arrangement with its creditors.

## 15. Stamp Duty and Costs {type=general}

15.1 The Tenant shall bear the stamp duty payable on this Lease and any renewal or variation of it. Each party bears its own legal costs.

## 16. Notices and Governing Law {type=general}

16.1 Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, registered post, or email to the addresses last notified.

16.2 This Lease is governed by the laws of Singapore.
