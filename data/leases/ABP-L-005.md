# Lease — Harbourline Tower, #08-03

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 10 July 2025 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Quillon Software Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Parties and Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant #08-03, Harbourline Tower, 8 Harbourline Quay, Singapore (the "Premises"), with a lettable area of approximately 2,100 square feet, fitted with the Landlord's partitions and carpet.

## 2. Term and Commencement {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is two (2) years from 1 August 2025 to 31 July 2027.

2.2 As the Premises are let in fitted condition, no rent-free fitting-out period applies.

## 3. Rent and Payment {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The rent is S$11.80 per square foot per month, being S$24,780.00 per month (the "Rent"), exclusive of GST, fixed for the term.

3.2 Rent is payable monthly in advance on the first day of each month.

## 4. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$1.25 per square foot per month (S$2,625.00 per month), which the Landlord may revise annually by written notice.

## 5. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to three (3) months' Rent and service charge, being S$82,215.00, on signing this Lease.

## 6. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

6.1 The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, license, or otherwise part with or share possession or occupation of the Premises or any part of it. This prohibition is absolute.

## 7. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

7.1 The Premises shall be used only as an office for software development and related services.

## 8. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

8.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises, including the Landlord's partitions and carpet, in good and clean condition, fair wear and tear excepted.

## 9. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

9.1 The Tenant shall not alter the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent.

9.2 On expiry, the Tenant shall return the Premises in the fitted condition in which they were let, and shall remove any additions made by the Tenant.

## 10. Insurance {type=insurance}

10.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$2,000,000 per occurrence.

## 11. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

11.1 The Landlord may sell the building subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, whereupon the Landlord is released from liability to refund it. The Tenant shall sign an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 12. Termination Events {type=termination}

12.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid for fourteen (14) days after its due date, if any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 13. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

13.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
