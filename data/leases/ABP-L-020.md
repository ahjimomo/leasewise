# Lease — Atlas Bay Commons, #01-05

*Fictional document for demonstration only. Not legal advice.*

This Lease is made on 8 April 2024 between **Atlas Bay Properties Pte Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Lanternfish Dental Clinic Pte Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Premises {type=parties_premises}

1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant unit #01-05, Atlas Bay Commons, 3 Atlas Bay Walk, Singapore (the "Premises"), with a lettable area of approximately 1,100 square feet.

## 2. Term {type=term_commencement}

2.1 The term is three (3) years from 1 May 2024 to 30 April 2027.

2.2 The Tenant shall have a rent-free fitting-out period of six (6) weeks from 1 May 2024.

## 3. Rent {type=rent_payment}

3.1 The Tenant shall pay rent of S$18.00 per square foot per month, being S$19,800.00 per month, exclusive of GST, monthly in advance and fixed for the term.

## 4. Service Charge {type=service_charge}

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of S$1.60 per square foot per month (S$1,760.00 per month), revisable annually on written notice.

## 5. Advertising and Promotion Charge {type=service_charge}

5.1 The Tenant shall pay an advertising and promotion charge of S$0.60 per square foot per month (S$660.00 per month).

## 6. Security Deposit {type=security_deposit}

6.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of S$66,660.00, equal to three (3) months' rent, service charge, and advertising and promotion charge.

## 7. Permitted Use {type=permitted_use}

7.1 The Premises shall be used only as a dental clinic providing general and cosmetic dentistry, operated by dentists registered in Singapore under a valid clinic licence, and for no other purpose.

7.2 The Tenant may dispense medicines to its own patients only. No other retail sales are permitted.

## 8. Assignment and Subletting {type=assignment_subletting}

8.1 The Tenant may assign this Lease to a purchaser of its dental practice with the Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Subletting is not permitted.

## 9. Fit-Out and Reinstatement {type=fit_out_reinstatement}

9.1 The Tenant shall fit out the Premises, including X-ray shielding and dental chair plumbing, in accordance with plans approved by the Landlord and all regulatory requirements.

9.2 At the end of the term the Tenant shall remove the X-ray equipment and shielding through a licensed contractor and reinstate the Premises to bare condition.

## 10. Repair and Maintenance {type=repair}

10.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises and its clinical installations in good repair and in a clean and hygienic condition.

## 11. Insurance {type=insurance}

11.1 The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$2,000,000 per occurrence and medical malpractice insurance for all practitioners.

## 12. Sale of the Property {type=landlord_sale}

12.1 The Landlord may sell Atlas Bay Commons subject to this Lease and transfer the security deposit to the purchaser, upon which the Landlord is released from liability for it. The Tenant shall provide an estoppel certificate within fourteen (14) days of request.

## 13. Termination Events {type=termination}

13.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease if rent is unpaid fourteen (14) days after its due date, if the Tenant's clinic licence is revoked or suspended for more than thirty (30) days, if any other breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 14. Retail Leasing Code {type=general}

14.1 The parties acknowledge that this Lease is a qualifying lease under the Lease Agreements for Retail Premises Act 2023 and has been prepared with reference to the Code of Conduct for Leasing of Retail Premises in Singapore.

## 15. Stamp Duty and Governing Law {type=general}

15.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Lease. This Lease is governed by Singapore law.
